## Master's Thesis

### Applying Data Science Techniques to Investigate Schedule Adherence in Maritime Transport

The objective of my thesis was to investigate how reliably major global shipping companies adhere to their published vessel schedules by analysing operational shipping data collected over several months.

To support this research, I designed an end-to-end analytical workflow covering automated data collection, data validation, exploratory analysis, and statistical modelling.

### Key Contributions

- Designed automated Python workflows to collect daily vessel schedule data across multiple global shipping companies over a three-month period.
- Structured and analysed large operational datasets using Python and SQL.
- Developed multiple internal **Streamlit applications** to accelerate data validation, cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory analysis, and anomaly detection throughout the research process.
- Applied clustering and statistical techniques to identify schedule adherence patterns and operational inefficiencies.
- Built interactive visualisations and analytical tools to communicate insights and support data-driven conclusions.
