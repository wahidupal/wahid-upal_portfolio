# About Me

My journey into data began through analytics, but over time I became increasingly interested in the engineering behind reliable data. I enjoy designing data models, building SQL solutions, automating repetitive workflows, developing interactive analytical applications, and improving data quality to help organizations make better decisions.

Alongside my professional experience, I completed my Master's degree at the **Hamburg University of Technology**, where my research focused on applying data science techniques to analyze maritime transport schedules. During this work I developed automated data collection pipelines, built Streamlit applications for data validation and analysis, and processed large-scale operational datasets using Python and SQL.

Whether I am building a SQL data warehouse, developing a Streamlit application, creating Power BI dashboards, or designing data transformation workflows, my goal is always the same: **turn complex data into reliable and meaningful information.**
